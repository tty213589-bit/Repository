Sabay Shop Support

App: Sabay Shop

For: Cambodian small businesses

Common help

Use Settings inside Sabay Shop to change the USD/KHR exchange rate, add a KHQR image, export a backup, or restore a backup.

If the app is reinstalled, restore your exported backup file to recover locally stored shop data.

For store-review support, contact the publisher through the contact information shown on the App Store or Google Play listing.
