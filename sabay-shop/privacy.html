Sabay Shop Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Sabay Shop is designed to store shop data locally on the user's device. The current version does not require an account and does not send sales, inventory, expense, customer, or KHQR image data to our servers.

Sabay Shop does not sell personal data and does not include advertising trackers. Users may export their own backup files. Those files remain under the user's control.

Sabay Shop does not request or store online-banking passwords, PINs, card credentials, or Telegram bot tokens. KHQR payments in the current version are confirmed manually by the shop owner.

If cloud sync or other online features are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Support: use the Sabay Shop support page linked from the app listing.
