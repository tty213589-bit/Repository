Sanctuary

Somewhere a conservatory library went quiet, and the glass roof let the forest back in. Sanctuary is that room, cast in aluminum and hand-painted glass — ivy climbing the shelves, gothic windows still standing, a halo of light where the skylight used to be. The chassis doesn't hide the machine inside; it frames it like a specimen worth keeping.
